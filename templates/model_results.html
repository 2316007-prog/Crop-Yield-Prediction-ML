Mean Absolute Error (MAE)

Measures the average magnitude of absolute prediction errors in target units (Metric Tonnes / Hectare). Random Forest achieves 1.01 MT/ha, meaning predictions deviate by barely 1 tonne per hectare on average.

📉

Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)

Penalizes larger outliers more heavily due to quadratic error squaring. At 3.56 MT/ha, Random Forest significantly outperforms Linear Regression (6.34 MT/ha), proving robust resilience against extreme variances.

📈

Coefficient of Determination (R²)

Quantifies the proportion of yield variance explained by environmental and agricultural inputs. An R² of 0.9106 demonstrates excellent predictive explanatory power across diverse Indian agro-climatic conditions.
